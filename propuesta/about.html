Cecilia Bautista

Visual Artist · Mexico City

Cecilia Bautista is a Mexican visual artist. Her practice is born from the desire to translate emotions that find no place in words. She works across a range of media — watercolour, oil, printmaking, glass and ceramics — exploring the expressive possibilities of each material and its capacity to hold the fragility of grief, memory and the beauty of the ephemeral.

She is drawn to making images and objects in which the viewer can recognise something of their own — in the fragmented, in what fades and in what transforms. Her work also functions as a vital tool for survival and re-signification, a way of recovering memories, everyday experiences and emotional processes.

Throughout her career she has taken part in exhibitions and projects across Mexico, including Palacio de Minería, Vórtice Galería, Biblioteca Pública del Estado de Jalisco, Casa de Cultura Santa María la Ribera, Casa de Cultura La Lata and the cultural space PRIM.
